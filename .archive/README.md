## Why This Archive Exists

During Phase 11 (Comprehensive Users Manual & Documentation), we consolidated scattered documentation into a organized, single-source-of-truth structure. The files in this archive were part of earlier phases and implementation notes but are no longer the authoritative source.

Benefits of the new structure:
- ✅ Single entry point (`DOCUMENTATION.md`)
- ✅ Clear navigation and cross-references
- ✅ Organized by use case and audience
- ✅ Reduced duplication
- ✅ Easier maintenance
